# OntoGEM

OntoGEM is an ontology for the representation, documentation, traceability, interoperability, and explainability of genome-scale metabolic models (GEMs) and the knowledge involved in their reconstruction and curation.

## About

The ontology aims to provide a formal semantic representation of the knowledge involved in constructing and maintaining genome-scale metabolic models. This includes not only biological knowledge incorporated into GEMs, but also model representations, modeling decisions, assumptions, evidence, methods, tools, and provenance.

OntoGEM distinguishes between:

1. **Biological reality** - biological entities and processes that exist independently of a computational model.
2. **Model representation** - how biological knowledge is represented within a metabolic model.
3. **Provenance and epistemic context** - information about how and why model elements were created, curated, modified, or supported by evidence.

## Scope

OntoGEM focuses on the knowledge involved in GEM reconstruction and curation, including:

- biological knowledge incorporated into metabolic models
- model representations and abstractions
- modeling decisions and assumptions
- evidence supporting model components
- reconstruction and curation activities
- methods and tools used during model development
- provenance and traceability of model elements

The goal is to support reproducibility, interoperability, traceability, validation, and explainability across GEM reconstruction and curation workflows.
